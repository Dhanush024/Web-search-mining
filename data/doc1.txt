Information Retrieval (IR) is the activity of obtaining information system resources that are relevant to an information need from a collection of those resources.
Searches can be based on full-text or other content-based indexing.
An automated information retrieval system is used to reduce what has been called information overload.
Search engines are the most common application of information retrieval methods.
In modern search engines, an inverted index is the core data structure enabling ultra-fast full-text searches.
When a user submits a query, the query processor searches the inverted index to find matching documents.
Ranking algorithms then sort the results so the most relevant documents appear at the top.
