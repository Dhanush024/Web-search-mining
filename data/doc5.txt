Vector space models represent both documents and user queries as multi-dimensional vectors in a high-dimensional vocabulary space.
The weight assigned to each term in a document vector is commonly determined by the TF-IDF weighting scheme.
Term frequency measures how frequently a term appears within a specific document, reflecting local relevance.
Inverse document frequency attenuates the weight of terms that appear very frequently across the entire collection, emphasizing rare and discriminatory terms.
To determine document relevance, the search engine computes cosine similarity between the query vector and document vectors.
Cosine normalization compensates for differences in document length, ensuring long documents do not dominate search results purely due to higher word counts.
Ranked retrieval returns documents sorted in descending order of their cosine similarity or BM25 relevance scores.
